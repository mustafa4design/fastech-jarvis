---

We cut a DTC brand's CPA from $47 to $19 in 4 weeks.

Same ad spend. Same audience targeting. Same product.

Different creative system.

---

They came to us running 3 creatives.

Same 3 creatives for 6 weeks straight.

The ROAS had been declining since week 3. Meta was fatiguing the audience. They thought the product had peaked.

It hadn't. The creative had.

---

We ran a brief intake on Monday night.

By Tuesday morning they had 8 new variants — each built from a different hook direction, all pulled from their top-performing organic content.

Not new concepts. Reframes of what was already working.

---

Week 1: We identified which 3 of the 8 were generating signal by day 3.

High CTR + strong dwell time in the first 4 seconds.

We paused the other 5.

Week 2: We rebuilt the 3 performers into full structured edits. Pattern interrupt in the first 2 seconds. Customer-language copy. No polished voiceover.

Week 3: Meta found a new audience cluster. The algorithm had surface area it didn't have before.

Week 4: CPA at $19. Down from $47.

---

CPA doesn't improve when you spend more.

It improves when you give the algorithm more variants to work with.

3 creatives is not a testing strategy. It's hope.

Meta's internal creative testing recommendation just jumped to 30+ variants per ad set.

Most DTC brands are still running 5.

---

This isn't a rumor. It's in Meta's Performance+ guidance now — built directly into the campaign interface.

They're telling you: the algorithm needs more surface area to find who converts.

---

The brands winning on Meta right now aren't better at making ads.

They're faster.

They can ship 30 creative variants before Monday because they have a system, not a department.

---

Here's what the math looks like if you don't have a system:

$300 cost-per-creative × 30 variants = $9,000 before you know what's working.

Most DTC brands are already paying this — they're just not getting 30 variants for it.

They're getting 5 polished ads, 3 rounds of revisions, and a 3-week turnaround.

That's not how the algorithm works anymore.

---

Meta's creative quality bar is dropping.

The authentic, rough-cut, pattern-interrupt ad is outperforming the polished brand video.

That means production cost is not the bottleneck.

Velocity is.

---

The agencies that understood this first shifted to:
- Overnight brief-to-delivery systems
- High-volume variant production (8–30 per campaign)
- Structured creative iteration instead of one-off productions

The ones that didn't are watching their clients' CPAs climb while the platform rewards their faster competitors.

---

The creative production bottleneck is now the primary cost driver in Meta performance — not the media buy.

If it takes you 2 weeks to produce 5 ads, Meta doesn't have what it needs to work.

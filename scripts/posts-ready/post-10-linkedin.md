Everyone in DTC is blaming Meta for their declining ROAS.

They're half right.

---

I've talked to 7 DTC brand owners in the last two weeks.

Same complaint every time. CPAs up. ROAS down. The algorithm "isn't working."

Meta IS more expensive than it was 18 months ago. That part is true.

But the campaigns that are still performing?

I've seen enough of them to notice what they have in common.

---

It's not a Meta problem.

It's a creative velocity problem disguised as a Meta problem.

---

The accounts that haven't seen a drop share one trait.

They're publishing 10–15 new creative variants per week.

Not per month. Per week.

They're not buying their way through the algorithm.

They're giving it enough raw material to find buyers.

---

Meta didn't break. It just got better at filtering out lazy creative.

The same 3 videos you've been running for 8 weeks used to work because there was less competition.

Now there's more.

The platform didn't change what it rewards. It just got faster at punishing the accounts that stopped feeding it.

---

Brief comes in overnight. Finished variants are in the client's queue by morning.

8 new creative variations per cycle. Different hooks, different pacing, different first-3-second treatments.

The clients on this system are holding CPA while the ones running static creative watch theirs climb.

Not because we have a magic formula.

Because we show up with new options faster than the algorithm exhausts the old ones.

The holiday season isn't won by the brands spending the most.

It's won by the brands testing the most.

November CPMs are up 35-50% right now.

I've been watching DTC founders react to that number all week.

Two completely different responses.

One group: cut budgets, pause campaigns, wait for things to calm down.

Other group: shipped 3 new creatives Monday morning.

Same platform. Same CPM environment. Different outcome.

Here's what I realized watching that split:

High CPMs don't hurt brands with deep creative pools.

They hurt brands with 1-2 ads carrying the entire account.

When you're testing 15-20 creatives per month, rising CPMs just filter out your weak ads faster.

It becomes a creative accelerant, not a threat.

The panic isn't about CPMs.

It's about not having enough creative variety to absorb the pressure.

Brands adjusting bids and cutting spend are treating the symptom.

The actual problem is creative supply.

Production velocity is the November moat.

Not budget. Not bidding.

What does your creative output look like right now?

My personal account gets 10x more reach than the FASTECH company page.

Same week. Same topics. Similar content.
One posted on the company page. One on my personal profile.

FASTECH company page: around 400 impressions.
My personal account: around 4,000.

Same audience. Same subject matter.
10x difference.

The algorithm doesn't treat logos and faces the same way.
People follow people. Companies don't have social networks.

A company page on LinkedIn exists. It's findable.
But the algorithm doesn't distribute it the way it distributes personal profiles.

Your personal profile does. Because that's where the actual network lives.

Here's the distinction that matters:

The company page is for credibility.
When someone checks out FASTECH after seeing my posts, the page needs to be there and look right.

My personal account is for reach.
That's where new people actually find the brand.

If you're a founder and you're only posting on the company page, you're leaving 10x the reach untouched. At minimum.

The personal brand is not a vanity project.
It's the distribution layer that the company page can't replace.

Stop treating them as the same thing.

Have you actually compared your personal vs company page reach data? What's your ratio?

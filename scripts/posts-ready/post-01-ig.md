My first client paid me $50.

I spent it on software, not food.

At 17, I was cold DMing brands on Instagram.
No portfolio. No case studies. No credibility.

Just a Google Doc I called a "proposal"
and a lot of time on my hands.

That first $50 didn't change my life.
But it told me something was possible.

3 years later: 40+ global clients.
FASTECH is real. The agency is real.
The clients are real.

The part nobody talks about is what happened between
$50 and the first $1,000.
And between $1,000 and where we are now.

It wasn't a viral post.
It wasn't a lucky break.

It was 18 months of working for almost free
while figuring out what I was actually selling.

If you're early in the game —
this is for you.

The $50 client was the most important one I ever had.

Follow for more of the real story.

I'm a video editor watching AI replace video editors.

Here's what I built instead.

---

6 months ago, I sat in my Karachi apartment watching a demo video.

AI took 30 minutes of raw footage.

Cut it. Graded it. Delivered a finished reel.

In 47 seconds.

I'd spent 3 years learning to do that manually.

---

I had two options.

Fight it. Refuse to change. Watch clients leave when they found something cheaper.

Or build it. Become the person who makes the tool.

I chose option 2.

---

That's what Multiplayer AI is.

A collaborative video editor where AI handles the timeline.

Humans handle the creative direction.

I'm not competing with AI.

I'm building on top of it.

---

The founders who win the next decade won't be the ones who ignored the tools threatening them.

They'll be the ones who turned the threat into the product.

---

Are you fighting the wave or learning to ride it?

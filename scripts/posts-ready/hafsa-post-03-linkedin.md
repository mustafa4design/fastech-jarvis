3 questions to ask any creative agency before you sign.

Not trick questions. The ones that show you whether they have a system — or whether they figure it out as they go.

---

**"Walk me through what happens after I send you a brief."**

You want a specific answer.

Not "we start editing" or "our team reviews it."

A specific answer sounds like: "We parse the brief for five categories, flag any gaps within an hour, build the production document overnight, and the editor starts with a decision map, not a guess."

If the answer is vague, the process is vague.

Vague processes produce revision cycles.

---

**"How long does a revision take — and why?"**

The "why" is the important part.

A good agency can tell you exactly where in their process a revision sits and what triggers it.

A revision that takes 3 days "because we're busy" is a scheduling problem.

A revision that takes 4 hours "because we isolate the variable that changed" is a system problem — solved.

---

**"What do you need from me for a brief to be complete?"**

If they don't have a list, they don't have a standard.

And if they don't have a standard, every brief you send is a starting-from-zero problem on their end and a revision on yours.

The answer should take under 2 minutes to give and cover 4–6 specific things.

---

You're not evaluating their portfolio.

You're evaluating their process.

The portfolio shows what they've done. The process shows what you'll get.

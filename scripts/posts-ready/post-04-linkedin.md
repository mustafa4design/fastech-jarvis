Clients send us a brief at 11 PM.

They get a finished ad by 8 AM.

I've done this 40+ times. It's not magic. Here's the intake layer that makes it repeatable.

---

Most agencies start editing when the brief arrives.

We don't.

The Overnight Engine starts with a different question: what does the editor need to know before they touch a single frame?

---

Here's what our intake layer does in the first 10 minutes:

The brief gets parsed for three things — hook direction, visual tone, and the product's main claim.

Our AI cross-references it against our reference ad library. Similar products. Similar audiences. What worked before.

A structured template auto-populates. Gaps get flagged. Missing info gets surfaced before editing starts.

---

The editor receives a production-ready document.

Not a paragraph. Not a voice memo. A decision map.

By the time they open the timeline, 80% of the ad is already decided.

Hook type. Pacing structure. Visual references. Cut rhythm.

The editing itself takes 2–3 hours. The brief intake takes 10 minutes.

That's why we can deliver overnight.

---

Most agencies are slow because they start editing before they've finished thinking.

We separated those two steps.

That's the whole system.

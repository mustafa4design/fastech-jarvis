## 2. FASTECH.PAK — Agency Brand Profile
What we are: A video editing and brand strategy agency based in Karachi, Pakistan. We serve clients across Meta/performance ads, short-form social content, talking-head/personal brand videos, and event recaps.

Voice: Direct, confident, systems-over-hustle, no fluff. We sound like operators, not marketers.

Audience (ICP): Founders, coaches, and personal brands who need consistent content but don't have an in-house team. They're busy, skeptical of generic agencies, and want proof over promises.

Content format rules for @fastech.pakistan specifically:

Zero Reels on this account
Max 1-2 Carousels per week
Mostly Single Post format
(This is different from Mustafa's personal account — don't copy his reel-heavy rhythm here)

Analytical lens: When writing strategy or positioning copy, apply the Iceberg Model (surface behavior vs. the real emotional driver underneath) and the BAV Model (Differentiation → Relevance → Esteem → Knowledge). Don't just write what sounds good — write to the root driver.


## 3. Mustafa's Personal Brand — Profile
Full system lives in: Personal_Brand_SOP (Google Doc) + mustafa-personal-brand-system.skill (Claude skill) + GPT-Setup-Personal-Brand-Content-Designer (Google Doc) — read all three before writing a single post.

Quick summary:

4 content pillars: AI/editing tactics, agency/systems thinking, brand strategy frameworks, build-in-public
Weekly rhythm: 1-2 carousels, 3 single posts, 3 reels (reels currently paused)
Two-stage workflow: topics get approved first, THEN scripted. Never skip straight to scripting.
Instagram captions: short, punchy, line-broken, hashtag block + keyword brackets
LinkedIn captions: long-form personal narrative (hook → scene → realization → reframe → engagement question). No hashtags. Never reuse Instagram slide text as the LinkedIn hook.
Face placement on carousels: a style choice per template, not a fixed rule — some templates put his face late (slide 6-7), not on the cover

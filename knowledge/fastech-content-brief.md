## Voice — The Most Important Section
### Sound like this
An operator explaining something to another operator. Direct. Specific. No fluff. Systems over hustle. Results over promises.
### Not like this
A marketing agency writing about marketing. No "we're passionate about helping brands grow." No buzzwords. No hype.
### The test
Read the post out loud. Does it sound like something a real person with real experience would say? Or does it sound like a caption someone copy-pasted from a marketing template?

If it's the second — rewrite it.
### Rules
Short sentences. One idea per line.
Numbers beat adjectives. ("68 leads last month" not "significant lead growth")
Specific beats general. ("fashion brand, Karachi/UAE market" not "one of our clients")
Never open with: "We're excited to..." / "We believe..." / "At FASTECH we..."
Close on mechanism or cost — not a tidy summary
CTA only when the post has earned it. One action. Never three.


## The Iceberg Rule (Read This Carefully)
Every post has two levels:

Surface behaviour — what the ICP is doing wrong Example: posting randomly, getting 50–200 views, wasting ad spend

Root driver — what they're actually feeling Example: fear of being invisible while competitors grow, shame at not knowing what's broken

Your job: Write to the root driver first. The surface behaviour is just how you name the problem. The root driver is what makes them stop scrolling.

The test: Does at least one line in your post make the reader feel something about their own situation — before it mentions FASTECH? If no — rewrite the opening.


## The BAV Rule (Sequence Matters)
Write in this order. Don't skip ahead.

Differentiation first — Make FASTECH feel meaningfully different before anything else. Nobody cares about our tips until they see us as different from every other agency.
Relevance — Does this post speak to what they're feeling RIGHT NOW?
Esteem — Proof. Real numbers. Specific outcomes. Not claims.
Knowledge — Educational content. Comes last, not first.

Common mistake: Leading with knowledge (algorithm tips, hook formulas) before establishing differentiation. That's writing content any agency could write. Don't do it.


## Format Rules — @fastech.pakistan
### What we post

No Reels on @fastech.pakistan. Zero. Reels are for Mustafa's personal account only.
### Single post caption structure
Hook (1–2 lines): Bold claim or named problem. Must work for a complete stranger.
Problem expand (2–4 lines): Make them feel understood. Don't solve yet.
Value (4–8 lines): Specific insight, data, or story. One idea, fully explored.
Close (1–2 lines): Mechanism or cost. CTA only if earned.
### Carousel structure
Slide 1: Tension-creating headline. Not a title — a hook.
Slides 2–7: One idea per slide. Bold header + 2–3 lines max.
Second-to-last slide: Pull quote or single reframe. No bullets. Let it breathe.
Last slide: One CTA. Specific. One action only.

## What Good Looks Like — Examples
### Strong hot take (ToF)
Your competitors aren't winning because they have better cameras. They're winning because they post like they have a system — and you post like you're hoping something sticks. The algorithm isn't punishing you. It's just reporting what's happening. Every week you post without a strategy is a week their retargeting pool grows and yours doesn't. That's the math. Not the motivation.

Why it works: Leads with differentiation (we think in systems). Speaks to the root driver (shame + invisibility fear). Never mentions FASTECH until the reader has already felt the problem.


### Strong proof post (MoF)
Fashion brand. Karachi/UAE market. Month 0: 2,100 followers, 800 average reach, zero ads. Month 3: 9,400 followers. $7.20 CPL. 68 qualified leads last month. What changed: not the product, not the budget, not the camera. The system behind the content. Strategy first. Edit with intent. Ads layered on top of organic — not instead of it. DM us "SYSTEM" and we'll show you what this looks like for your brand.

Why it works: Specific numbers. Leads with the result. Names the mechanism without being preachy. CTA is earned.


### Strong positioning post (BoF)
We cap at 12 clients. Not because we can't handle more. Because at 13 we start cutting corners. 48-hour turnaround becomes 5 days. Strategy becomes template. Reporting becomes slides with the good numbers. We've seen what happens to agencies that scale fast. We'd rather be the one your brand remembers as the team that actually gave a damn. 2 spots open this month. Book the call in bio.

Why it works: Differentiator framed as identity, not feature. Specific number (13, not "too many clients"). Closes on cost — not on a tidy pitch.

## Quick Check Before You Post
Run through this before finalising any post:

Does the hook work for a complete stranger who has never heard of FASTECH?
Does at least one line make the reader feel something about their own situation?
Is FASTECH the tool, not the hero?
Does the close land on mechanism or cost — not a summary?
Could another agency have written this? If yes — rewrite until it couldn't.
Is there more than one CTA? If yes — cut to one.
Am I posting a Reel to @fastech.pakistan? If yes — stop. Wrong account.
